##### Getting Started
Welcome to Wildbloom
Wildbloom is magic made from flowers. Flowers make mana, pools store it, and you spend it to infuse items, power machines and charge gear.
Your first 30 minutes
1. Find or craft a Dawnpetal (Dandelion + Bone Meal) and a Tidebloom (Blue Orchid + Bone Meal). Wild ones grow in plains, forests, rivers and swamps.
2. Craft a Small Mana Pool: stone in a U shape with a Dawnpetal in the middle (see Crafting Recipes).
3. Place the pool, then plant flowers within 6 blocks of it. Dawnpetals need open sky and daylight. Tideblooms need a water block touching them.
4. Make a Mana Wand (Dawnpetal on two sticks). Sneak and right-click anything from Wildbloom to read it. Right-click a pool with an empty hand to see its mana.
5. Drop an Iron Ingot onto the pool. Once the pool holds 3,000 mana it turns into a Mana Ingot. That is how all pool infusion works: drop the items on top and wait.
6. Turn stone into Livingrock and logs or any planks into Livingwood Planks the same way, then craft a full Mana Pool. A Mana Diamond needs 8,000 mana, more than a Small Mana Pool holds (5,000).
Signs to watch for
Green sparkles from a flower: it just sent mana to a pool.
Grey smoke from a flower: it made mana but no pool within 6 blocks had room.
Grey smoke from a pool: it sees your items but needs more mana. A message tells you how much.
Read the Progression chapter next for the full road from your first flower to the Heartwood Warden.

##### Progression
Stage 1: First mana
Dawnpetal, Tidebloom, Small Mana Pool, Mana Wand.
Goal: a pool that refills on its own.
Stage 2: Infusion basics
Infuse Livingrock (stone, 500), Livingwood Planks (any log for 4, or any planks one for one, 125 each) and Mana Ingots (iron, 3,000).
Craft a Mana Pool (Small Mana Pool ringed with Livingrock), a Mana Spreader, Livingwood gear and your first Mana tools.
Plant a Living Sapling for a tree that makes mana.
Stage 3: More flowers, first runes
Make the Mana Ingot flowers: Hungerbloom, Nightshade, Mossbloom, Coalbloom, Harvestbloom, Smithbloom, and the first helper flowers.
Craft a Rune Altar and make the four elemental runes (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, 4,000 each).
Infuse a Mana Diamond (diamond, 8,000, needs a Mana Pool).
Stage 4: Diamond flowers and the first boss
Mana Diamond flowers (Emberbloom, Frostbloom, Soulbloom and the rest) roughly double your income.
Mine Mana Crystal Ore between Y=0 and Y=-60.
Craft the Ritual Altar and set 4 pools around it.
Make a Crystal Idol and summon the Crystal Sentinel (10,000 mana).
Stage 5: Verdant tier
Verdant Alloy (Rune Altar, 12,000) unlocks Verdant tools, armor and the Verdant Bow.
Make seasonal runes and a Thorn Seed, then fight the Thornmother (20,000 mana). She drops more Verdant Alloy.
Upgrade to a Grand Mana Pool (holds 100,000).
Stage 6: Fae Steel and the sins
Fae Steel (Rune Altar, 30,000, needs Netherite Scrap) unlocks the Terra Blade, Gale Glaive, Fae Shovel, Netherbloom, Voidbloom and the Warp Bloom.
Make all seven sin runes (16,000 each).
Stage 7: The Heartwood Warden
Craft the Warden Sigil (60,000 at the Rune Altar) and summon the Heartwood Warden (100,000 mana from the 4+ pools around the Ritual Altar). It drops Heartwood for the best tools and armor in the mod.

=== How mana flows
Filling
Every flower and generator sends its mana to the nearest pool within 6 blocks. When that pool is full, the rest goes to the next nearest. Pools can be above, below or beside the flower.
Spending
Machines, altars and helper flowers take mana from their own small buffer first (filled by Mana Spreaders), then from pools around them: 6 blocks for altars and machines, 8 for helper flowers and the Elevator. They can add up several pools together.
Reading a pool
Right-click a pool with an empty hand, or sneak-right-click it with the Mana Wand. The Mana Gauge shows the fill of a pool within 2 blocks as a column of sparkles (6 = full).
Overflow pools
Once over 90% full, an Overflow Mana Pool pushes 500 mana a second into every pool touching it. Put it in the middle of your flowers and chain normal pools to it.

=== Charging gear
Charging and repairing
Drop any mana tool, weapon, battery or the Mana Band onto a pool. Every second it gains up to 1,000 charge and repairs up to 25 durability (2 mana per point).
Tool charge shows in the item's tooltip. Mana tools never break: each block you mine costs 10 charge instead of durability. Mana swords cost 10 charge per hit the same way. With no charge they wear down almost to nothing and stop there; they never break. At that point they work like your bare hand (slow mining, 1 damage per hit) until you charge them again. Mana, Verdant and Heartwood armor also runs on charge (see Armor).
Other ways
Mana Battery / Tablet: right-click a pool to empty the battery into it, sneak-right-click to fill the battery (up to 5,000 per click).
Mana Mirror: charges the gear in your off hand from a bound pool up to 64 blocks away.
Mana Band (off hand): feeds 300 charge every half second into the mana gear in your main hand.
Full Livingwood armor: slowly charges the mana gear in your hand.

##### Moving Mana  [Spreaders and relays | Lenses | Sparks | Back]
Spreaders, relays, prisms, gates, lenses and sparks.

=== Spreaders and relays
Mana Spreader
Put it touching a pool. Every second it pulls up to 600 mana from the pool and fires a 300 mana pulse in the direction it faces, up to 24 blocks. Right-click it with the Mana Wand to turn it (up, down, then the four sides).
The pulse flies through air, water and non-solid blocks and stops at the first solid block. If that block is a pool, relay, prism, gate, machine or altar, it receives the mana. Anything else wastes the pulse.
Pulses lose 1% per block travelled (never more than half).
Mana Relay
Catches pulses and fires them on in the direction it faces. Use relays to go around corners or past 24 blocks. Holds 2,000.
Mana Prism
Like a relay but splits each pulse three ways: forward and to both sides (or east and west if it faces up or down).
Mana Gate
A relay that only fires while it has a redstone signal. Use it to switch a line on and off.
Sun Mirror
A spreader with its own power: in daylight under open sky it gathers 40 mana a second and fires it like a spreader. No pool needed.

=== Lenses
Right-click a Spreader, Relay, Gate or Sun Mirror with a lens to fit it. Fitting a new lens gives back the old one.

Velocity Lens
Range 48 instead of 24.
shapeless: Glass Pane + Sugar + Mana Ingot

Weight Lens
Fires 900 mana pulses (3x), but only every 2 seconds.
shapeless: Glass Pane + Iron Block + Mana Ingot

Bounce Lens
When the pulse hits a block that can't take mana, it bounces straight back once instead of being wasted, so it can reach a pool or relay on the far side of the spreader.
shapeless: Glass Pane + Slime Ball + Mana Ingot

Piercing Lens
The pulse passes through the first wrong block. Good for shooting through one wall.
shapeless: Glass Pane + Arrow + Mana Ingot

Pool Filter Lens
Only delivers to pools. The pulse ignores machines and relays it hits.
shapeless: Glass Pane + Lapis Lazuli + Mana Ingot

=== Sparks
Mana Spark
Right-click a pool with a Spark to attach it. Every second, sparked pools within 12 blocks of each other even out how full they are (up to 400 mana a second per pair). No line of sight needed.
Use Sparks to feed the Ritual Altar's 4 pools from one big pool, or to share mana between a flower farm and your workshop.

How to make
Crafting table: shapeless: Mana Ingot + Blaze Powder

##### Helper Flowers  [Reaper | Collector | Herder | Smelter | Sorter | Logger | Tiller | Miner | Lamp Bloom | Warden Bloom | Mender | Healer | Feeder | Warp Bloom | Seeder | Quickbloom | Back]
Helper flowers do a job every 1 second and pay for it from pools within 8 blocks. If the pools are empty they stop. A helper's range reaches just as far up and down as it does sideways, so you can tuck it under a floor or farm. Sneak-right-click one with the Wand to see its range and cost.

=== Reaper
Reaper
Harvests fully grown crops within 5 blocks in every direction (up and down too, so it works planted under the farm) and replants them. It also cuts sugar cane taller than 1 block, leaving the bottom block to regrow, up to 4 a second. The drops fall on the ground, so pair it with a Collector. 8 mana per crop.

How to make
Pool infusion: drop Harvestbloom + Mana Ingot + Iron Hoe into a pool holding 4,000 mana (Small Mana Pool or bigger).

=== Collector
Collector

##### Bosses  [Crystal Sentinel | Thornmother | Heartwood Warden | Back]
Three bosses, summoned at the Ritual Altar. Each one drops what you need for the next. Fight them in this order.

=== Crystal Sentinel
Crystal Sentinel
Health: 300   Melee: 8

Summoning
Use a Crystal Idol at the Ritual Altar (10,000 mana).
Rune Altar: 3x Mana Crystal + Rune of Water + Mana Diamond, 12,000 mana.

The fight
- Every 5 seconds it fires crystal shards at every player within 16 blocks (6 damage each).
- At two thirds and one third health it raises 3 amethyst crystals around itself and becomes nearly immune to damage. Break all three crystals, then keep attacking.

Strategy
Bring a pickaxe for the crystals and a few golden apples. Fight it in the open so the crystals spawn where you can reach them.

Drops
  4-8x Mana Crystal
  1x Rune of Water

=== Thornmother
Thornmother
Health: 600   Melee: 10

Summoning
Use a Thorn Seed at the Ritual Altar (20,000 mana).
Rune Altar: Rune of Earth + Rune of Spring + Vine + Sweet Berries, 10,000 mana.

The fight
- Every 6 seconds roots burst out under players within 12 blocks: 4 damage and Slowness III.
- Every 7 seconds she calls silverfish (up to 6 at a time).
- She is weak to fire: every fire or lava hit deals 8 extra damage, and while she burns she takes 6 more damage twice a second.

Strategy
Put Fire Aspect on your sword (Rune of Fire at the Mana Enchanter) or Flame on a bow, or lead her into lava. Stay out of the 12 block root range when you can.

Drops
  1-3x Verdant Alloy
  1x Thorn Seed
  1-2x Rune of Earth

=== Heartwood Warden
Heartwood Warden
Health: 1,500   Melee: 16

Summoning
Use a Warden Sigil at the Ritual Altar (100,000 mana).
Rune Altar: Fae Steel + Mana Diamond + Rune of Pride + Rune of Greed + Rune of Lust + Rune of Envy + Rune of Gluttony + Rune of Wrath + Rune of Sloth, 60,000 mana.

The fight
- Phase 1 (full to two thirds health): every 6 seconds it slams the ground, 10 damage and a big knockback to anyone within 6 blocks.
- Phase 2 (two thirds to one third): every 4 seconds lightning strikes beside each player within 24 blocks.
- Phase 3 (below one third): every 5 seconds it dashes up to 6 blocks at you, and it calls Warden Echoes (husks, up to 4).

Strategy
Fight at range in phase 1 (Verdant Bow), wear a Lava Ring in phase 2 so the fires don't hurt, and save your Terra Blade for phase 3. A Stormbloom near the arena turns phase 2 into free mana.

Drops
  4-8x Heartwood
  1-2x Fae Steel
  2-4x Mana Diamond

##### Armor
Wear all four pieces of one set for its bonus. Armor is crafted like vanilla armor from the set's material.

Mana, Verdant and Heartwood armor never breaks. Each piece holds charge (Mana 20,000, Verdant 50,000, Heartwood 100,000) and damage you take is paid from that charge instead of durability. A piece with no charge wears down almost to nothing and then stops protecting you until you charge it again: drop it on a pool. Livingwood armor wears out and breaks like vanilla armor. Enchants work on all mana gear: Unbreaking makes it use charge less often, and Sharpness, Efficiency, Protection and the rest work as normal. Gear with no charge loses its enchants' help too.

Livingwood armor  7 armor points total
  Full set bonus: Slowly charges the mana gear in your main hand (20 a second).

Mana armor  15 armor points total
  Full set bonus: Pulls dropped items within 5 blocks to you.

Verdant armor  20 armor points total
  Full set bonus: Night Vision and Slow Falling.

Heartwood armor  24 armor points total
  Full set bonus: Water Breathing, Absorption II and Speed.

##### Exploring
Wild flowers
Dawnpetals grow on grass in plains and forests. Tideblooms grow on grass by rivers, in swamps and in forests.

Mana Crystal Ore
Found in stone and deepslate between Y=0 and Y=-60. Drops 1-3 Mana Crystals. Crystals make the Crystal Idol and the Crystal Heart.

Wild Gardens
A Small Mana Pool (with a little mana in it) ringed by flowers, with a chest of Dawnpetals and Tideblooms, and sometimes a Mana Ingot, a Mana Wand or a Lexicon. A free start if you find one.

Overgrown Shrines
Livingrock ruins guarded by a zombie or skeleton. The chest holds an elemental rune, 2-4 Mana Crystals, a tier 2 flower, Mana Ingots, and sometimes a Thorn Seed.

Gardens and shrines appear on open ground around you as you explore new land.

##### Help & FAQ
My altar, brewery or enchanter does nothing
If it smokes and shows a message, the items are right but it needs more mana: the message shows how much it has and needs. It uses its own stored mana or the pools within 6 blocks added together. No smoke means the items don't match a recipe yet.

My pool won't craft
Drop all the items close together on top of the pool. If the pool smokes, it needs more mana; the message shows how much. If it says the pool is too small, use a bigger pool (Mana Diamond needs a Mana Pool).

My flower smokes
No pool within 6 blocks has room. Add a pool or empty one.

My flower does nothing
Sneak-right-click it with the Wand to see what it needs, and check its page in Generating Flowers.

My spreader doesn't send mana
It must touch a pool that has mana, face the target (turn it with the Wand) and have a clear line under 24 blocks. Solid blocks in the way stop the pulse.

Machines stop
They need mana in their buffer or in pools within 6 blocks (8 for helper flowers).

I lost my Lexicon
Craft a new one: Book + Oak Sapling.

Is my pack up to date?
When you join a world, chat shows "Wildbloom vX loaded". If it's an old number, Minecraft is using a cached copy: remove the old pack and import the new one.
